The Journal

  • 6-8 page maximum

  • Will be reviewed by at least two faculty members from the EE and/or CS departments

  • Suggested content: excerpts or modifications of longer papers that were submitted to other journals, drafts of papers to be submitted to proper technical journals

  • Papers should be prepared in LaTeX; template files are available on the ECJ website at http://ieee.stanford.edu/ecj/templates.html

  • Pictures/images must be in CMYK (not RGB) color format

  • MATLAB plots and other figures must be in EPS format
