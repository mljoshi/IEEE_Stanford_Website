Review Process for Long Papers

1) All papers submitted will appear with a timestamp in this directory.

2) Albert will announce all new paper submissions on this email list, and you will have 24 hours to "sign up" for a paper whose topic falls under your background, interest or expertise. If a paper goes unclaimed, Albert will make an arbitrarily assignment. In either case, once a paper is assigned to one of us, it will move into that individual's folder within the submissions/ directory. For example, a paper I am overseeing will go in http://ieee/ecj/submissions/clara. It will still be viewable by the rest of us.

3) Please review papers to which you have been assigned in a timely manner. Work with the author on typos and any blatant readability or grammatical errors. At the same time (as soon as you are assigned the paper, in fact), find professor(s) and others who will be willing to review the paper.

4) Provide the secondary readers with paper versions of [1] the latest revision of the paper, with authors' names fully crossed out and [2] the review form at http://ieee/ecj/submissions/review%20sheet.pdf. Follow up with them to ensure you receive their comments by Monday, May 5th.

5) Attend the May 5th steering committee meeting (in Gates 104, as usual). It is imperative that you attend this meeting, as we will be making our final paper selections at this time.

6) Work with the author to make all necessary changes by Friday, May 16th.

Review Process for Short Papers (No faculty review)

Steps 1 and 2 as above

3) Work with the author on typos and any blatant readability or grammatical errors.

4) Find at least one external reader who is technical but not necessarily expert in the field. Short papers are meant to be survey papers for the lay technical public. Provide the secondary readers with paper versions of [1] the latest revision of the paper, with authors' names fully crossed out and [2] the review form at http://ieee/ecj/submissions/review%20sheet.pdf. Follow up with them to ensure you receive their comments by Monday, May 5th.

5) Attend the May 5th steering committee meeting (in Gates 104, as usual). It is imperative that you attend this meeting, as we will be making our final paper selections at this time.

6) Work with the author to make all necessary changes by Friday, May 16th.
